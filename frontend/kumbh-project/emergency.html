🛡️ Safety Tips for Pilgrims

📱 Save Numbers

Screenshot this page or write down emergency numbers before arriving at the mela grounds.

👦‍👧 Keep Children Safe

Write your phone number on children's wrists. Use the Child Helpline (1098) if separated.

🏥 Medical Kits

Carry basic medicines (ORS, fever, BP). First-aid posts are available every 500m at the mela.

🔵 Blue Badges

Kumbh Seva volunteers wear blue badges. Approach them for any non-emergency assistance.

💧 Hydration

Drink at least 3 liters of water daily. Heat stroke is common. Free water kiosks are available.

🔍 If Lost

Go to the nearest Information Center (yellow signboards) or call the Kumbh Helpline: 1800-233-0000.
