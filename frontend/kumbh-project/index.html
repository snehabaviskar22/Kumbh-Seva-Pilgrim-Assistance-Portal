A Sacred Journey on the Godavari

Explore the sights, rituals, and spirit of Nashik Kumbh Mela 2027

Ram Kund – The Holiest Bath

Millions gather at this sacred spot where Lord Rama bathed. Bathing here during Kumbh is said to liberate the soul from the cycle of rebirth.

The Sacred Godavari River

Called the “Ganga of the South”, the Godavari carries the mythical Amrita — drops of immortal nectar that fell here during the cosmic battle of gods.

Panchavati – Where Ram Lived

The sacred forest of Panchavati in Nashik is where Lord Rama spent years of his exile. Every lane carries echoes of the Ramayana.

Tapovan – Land of Sages

Serene Tapovan where ancient rishis performed centuries of penance. A place of spiritual retreat and morning yoga by the river.

Ganga Aarti at Dusk

As the sun sets over the Godavari, the daily Aarti transforms the ghats into a glowing tapestry of fire, flowers, and devotion.

About Nashik Kumbh Mela

The Simhastha Kumbh at Nashik–Trimbakeshwar — one of four sacred Kumbh Melas held in India

🌊

The Sacred Godavari

Known as the “Ganga of the South”, the Godavari flows through Nashik. A holy dip during Kumbh is believed to wash away all sins and liberate the soul from birth and death.

📚

Mythological Significance

According to legend, drops of Amrita (nectar of immortality) fell at four places during the cosmic battle between gods and demons — Nashik being one, making it supremely sacred.

🕎

Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga

Home to one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas. Trimbakeshwar is where the Godavari originates. Pilgrims visit both Nashik and Trimbakeshwar for complete blessings during Kumbh.

🏰

Shahi Snan (Royal Baths)

On specific lunar dates, grand processions of saints and Akharas march to the ghats in full regalia. The three Shahi Snan dates draw the largest crowds of the entire Kumbh.

🧘

Akharas & Saints

13 major Akharas participate in Nashik Kumbh. Naga sadhus, Vaishnav saints, and thousands of seers gather for ancient rituals, discourses, and spiritual gatherings.

🎉

Cultural Celebrations

Beyond the spiritual — folk music, religious discourses, art exhibitions, food from across India, and the nightly Aarti make Nashik Kumbh an unforgettable experience.
