Sacred Ghats of Nashik

The holy bathing ghats on the Godavari — interactive map with real locations

The 6 Sacred Ghats

Click any ghat card to locate it on the interactive map below

Ghat 1 Primary Snan

Ram Kund

📍 Panchavati, Nashik

The most sacred ghat where Lord Rama bathed during exile. Primary site for all Shahi Snan processions. Water here is believed to never decompose.

Primary Snan Lord Rama Shahi Snan
📍 City Centre • 20.0033°N Directions →
Ghat 2

Lakshman Kund

📍 Near Ram Kund, Panchavati

Adjacent to Ram Kund, dedicated to Lord Lakshmana. Known for pristine clarity year-round. A peaceful spot for pilgrims after the main Ram Kund bath.

Lord Lakshmana Ramayana Sacred Bath
📍 50m from Ram Kund Directions →
Ghat 3 Meditation Zone

Tapovan

📍 Tapovan Road, Nashik

Serene forest-hermitage where sages Agastya and Valmiki meditated. Preferred by sadhus and yoga practitioners. Calm and away from main crowds.

Meditation Hermitage Peaceful
📍 2km from city Directions →
Ghat 4 Women Pilgrims

Sita Gufa

📍 Panchavati, Nashik

Dedicated to Goddess Sita, where she performed her daily rituals. Specially venerated by women pilgrims, with the Sita Temple overlooking the Godavari.

Goddess Sita Women Pilgrims Temple
📍 Near Ram Kund Directions →
Ghat 5 Maratha Heritage

Ramkund Ahilyadevi Ghat

📍 Panchavati, Nashik

Built by Maratha Queen Ahilyabai Holkar in the 18th century. Beautiful architecture hosts major Akhara processions during Shahi Snan ceremonies.

Ahilyabai Holkar Maratha Heritage Akhara
📍 Attached to Ram Kund Directions →
Ghat 6 Shiva Devotees

Kapaleshwar Ghat

📍 Old Nashik, Nashik

Named after sage Kapila, this ghat houses the ancient Kapaleshwar Mahadev temple. Major site for Shaiva devotees and Mahashivratri celebrations during Kumbh.

Sage Kapaleshwar Shiva Temple Shaiva Pilgrims
📍 1.5km from Ram Kund Directions →
