Complete Event Timeline

July 2027 – Opening

July 10, 2027 (Guru Purnima)
🕉 Kumbh Mela Inauguration
Official opening ceremony with lighting of the sacred lamp, processions of all 13 Akharas, and puja at Ram Kund by the Chief Minister of Maharashtra.
Opening Ceremony
July 21, 2027 (Shravan Shukla Pratipada)
🔥 FIRST SHAHI SNAN
The first and most auspicious Royal Bath. Naga sadhus lead the procession in magnificent formation. Millions bathe at Ram Kund from 4 AM. VIP areas accessible by pass only.
🔥 Shahi Snan #1
July 24, 2027 (Nag Panchami)
🐍 Nag Panchami Snan
Special bathing day for Nag Panchami. Serpent deity worship at all ghats. Processions of saints with live snake rituals at the Kapileshwar temple.
Special Snan

August 2027 – Peak Days

August 5, 2027 (Shravan Purnima / Raksha Bandhan)
🔥 SECOND SHAHI SNAN
The second grand Royal Bath coincides with Raksha Bandhan. Vaishnavas and Shaivas both participate. This is expected to draw the largest single-day crowd of 5+ million pilgrims.
🔥 Shahi Snan #2
August 9–12, 2027
📖 Spiritual Discourses & Satsang
4-day back-to-back spiritual discourses by eminent saints. Bhajans, kirtans, and Ram Katha by renowned Kathakars at the Central Stage near Tapovan.
Spiritual Programs
August 19, 2027 (Bhadrapad Amavasya)
🔥 THIRD SHAHI SNAN
The final grand Royal Bath of the 2027 Kumbh. All Akharas participate in a unified procession. Special midnight snan allowed this day only. Massive fireworks display at conclusion.
🔥 Shahi Snan #3

September 2027 – Closing

September 1– 5, 2027
🌿 Valedictory Processions
Final processions of all Akharas back to their home bases. Cultural programs, folk performances, and the last ritual Godavari aarti of the Kumbh 2027.
Closing Events
September 10, 2027 (Anant Chaturdashi)
🕉 Kumbh Mela Closing Ceremony
Grand finale with symbolic immersion ceremony, final mass prayer at Ram Kund, distribution of Prasad, and official closing address. Until Nashik Kumbh 2039.
Closing Ceremony

⚠️ Important Notes for Shahi Snan Days

  • • Arrive at the ghat area by 3 AM on Shahi Snan days for best access
  • • Large bags and vehicles are not permitted within 2km of ghats on Snan days
  • • Keep children close and note emergency contact numbers (Police: 100)
  • • Carry sufficient water, medicines, and identification documents
  • • Follow all crowd management instructions from police and volunteers
