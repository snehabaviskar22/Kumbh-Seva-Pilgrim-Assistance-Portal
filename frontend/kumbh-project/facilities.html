Pilgrim Facilities

Medical, water, transport, and accommodation — everything for a safe pilgrimage

Essential Facilities

🏥

Medical Centers

24/7 hospitals & first-aid posts throughout the mela grounds

💧

Drinking Water

Free potable water kiosks every 500m. Tanker supply during peak days

🏧

ATM & Banking

50+ ATMs deployed. Mobile banking vans near all major ghats

🩹

First Aid

150 first-aid booths. Trained volunteers at all junctions

🔒

Security

10,000+ police deployed. CCTV surveillance across mela area

🚻

Sanitation

5,000+ mobile toilets. Dedicated for women & disabled

🍱

Community Kitchens

Bhandara (free meals) served by various trusts and Akharas daily

ℹ️

Information Centers

Tourist desks in Hindi, Marathi, English at all entry points

How to Reach Nashik

✈️ Nashik Airport (Ozar)

06:00 – 08:00

Morning Flights from Mumbai / Pune

SpiceJet, IndiGo operate morning services. Pre-book airport shuttle to Ram Kund.

10:00 – 14:00

Midday Arrivals

Air India and Vistara connect Nashik from Delhi, Hyderabad, Bengaluru via Mumbai.

18:00 – 20:00

Evening Flights

Evening flights from major metros. Airport is 20km from Ram Kund (∼45 min).

Transfer

Airport → Ram Kund Ghat

Prepaid taxi: ₹400-600 | Shared cab: ₹80-120 | Kumbh bus: ₹30

🚂 Nashik Road Railway Station

04:30

Panchvati Express (from Mumbai CST)

Most popular pilgrim train. Journey ∼4h. Book months ahead during Kumbh period.

07:15

Devgiri Express (Aurangabad)

Connects Nashik from Aurangabad, Manmad. Ideal for Marathwada pilgrims.

11:30

Mumbai–Manmad Passenger

Economy option. Special Kumbh coaches added on peak Shahi Snan days.

Transfer

Nashik Rd Station → Ram Kund

City bus: ₹15 | Auto: ₹120-150 | Taxi: ₹250-350 | Distance: 6km

🚌 MSRTC Bus Services

Hourly

Mumbai → Nashik (MSRTC)

Shivshahi & Volvo buses hourly from Dadar, Borivali, Thane. Duration 3.5-4.5h. ₹280-480.

Every 2h

Pune → Nashik

Semi-luxury via Shirdi route. Duration ∼4h. Fare ₹320-520.

Special

Kumbh Special Buses

Maharashtra Govt deploys 2000+ extra buses from all cities on Shahi Snan dates.

Transfer

Bus Stand → Ram Kund

City bus: ₹10 | Auto: ₹80-100 | Distance: 3km (∼20 min)

🚺 Local Transport Options

🚺

Auto Rickshaw

Available everywhere. Meter fare + 20% Kumbh surcharge. Min ₹30.

🚌

City Bus

NMC buses connect all major ghats. Frequency increased. Fare ₹5-20.

⚡

E-Rickshaw

Eco-friendly. Seating 6. Operating in Panchavati. Fare ₹10-20.

🚖

Shared Taxi

Fixed routes on main corridors. Affordable group travel. ₹20-50 per person.

🚶

Walking Paths

Dedicated pedestrian corridors between all ghats. Well-lit & safe.

🏍️

Bike Taxi

Rapido & Ola Bike for quick navigation. ₹20-80.

Accommodation

Book in advance — capacity fills fast during Shahi Snan dates
